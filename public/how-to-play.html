Become an Influencer is a live multiplayer party game where everyone creates a ridiculous online personality and competes for imaginary fame. One person hosts the game. Everyone else joins on their own phone, posts to a shared feed, completes challenges, and votes for the ultimate influencer.

New here? Start with hosting if you're organizing the game, or joining if someone has already invited you.

PART ONE

HOW TO HOST A GAME

1

OPEN THE GAME

Visit become-an-influencer.onrender.com on your phone, tablet, or computer. On the home screen, scroll to HOST A PARTY. You do not need to sign up for an account.

2

CHOOSE A GAME MODE AND LENGTH

Choose Quick Fame for a short party game (20, 35, or 65 minutes total), or Event Mode for a longer experience (2, 4, 6, or 8 hours). In Event Mode, you can optionally set a scheduled start time or start it yourself. Choose a length and tap CREATE NEW GAME →.

Quick Fame includes five minutes beyond the length shown in the selection menu. Event Mode can be extended by the host during play.

3

INVITE YOUR PLAYERS

You'll arrive in THE CONTROL ROOM, your private host screen. It displays your room code and a QR code. Have guests scan the QR code with their phones, or tap COPY TEXT INVITATION ↗ and send the link in a group message. Remote players can join too.

Guests enter their influencer handle, choose a persona, and tap JOIN THE FEED →. Watch their names appear in your player list.

4

START THE GAME

When everyone is ready, tap START 20-MIN GAME, START 35-MIN GAME, START 65-MIN GAME, or START EVENT MODE, depending on your selection. The game begins, and the first challenge asks players to introduce their influencer identity.

If you scheduled an Event Mode start, the game can also begin automatically at that time while the server is running.

5

RUN THE PARTY

Challenges advance automatically as the game progresses. You can also tap NEXT CHALLENGE to move ahead. Want extra chaos? Use ⚡ SURPRISE DROP to send everyone a surprise challenge or post request without changing the scheduled rounds.

Tap OPEN PROJECTOR VIEW ↗ to show the live feed, QR code, challenges, and leaderboard on a shared screen. For Event Mode, you can also extend the game by 30 or 60 minutes.

6

START THE FINAL VOTE

When you're ready to wrap up, tap START FINAL VOTE. The game also moves to voting automatically when its time runs out. Players choose who deserves the crown; they cannot vote for themselves. Voting lasts 90 seconds in Quick Fame or 10 minutes in Event Mode.

7

REVEAL THE WINNER AND START AGAIN

The winner is revealed automatically when voting closes. The game uses final votes to determine the winner, with follower score as a tiebreaker. To organize another round, return to the home screen and create a new game. RESET GAME is a separate control for restarting the current room, not the button for revealing the winner.

PART TWO

HOW TO PLAY

1

JOIN YOUR HOST'S ROOM

Scan the host's QR code or tap their invitation link. If you opened the home screen yourself, enter the room code under JOIN A GAME. Choose your influencer handle and influencer type, then tap JOIN THE FEED →. Wait for the host to start.

2

CREATE YOUR RIDICULOUS INFLUENCER

Are you a luxury influencer with no money, a wellness guru with questionable advice, or a conspiracy creator with an inexplicably devoted audience? Your first challenge is to introduce your persona. Make it memorable.

3

CREATE AND PUBLISH POSTS

Use the Create area to write a caption, optionally attach a photo or video, and tap PUBLISH ↗. You can follow the current challenge or post something entirely your own. Photos and videos must be no larger than 8 MB each. Your posts appear in the shared feed.

Example: “My luxury morning routine begins with a $400 glass of tap water.”

4

LIKE, COMMENT, AND CLIMB THE LEADERBOARD

Scroll through everyone's posts. Give likes and leave comments on other players' content. Your engagement affects your followers, credibility, imaginary earnings, and notoriety. Your two highest-engagement posts per round count toward your score. Posting more won't replace a better-performing post just because it's newer.

5

HANDLE THE ALGORITHM

Watch for breaking influencer news, brand deals, public-relations disasters, and surprise challenges. Choose how your influencer responds. Your decisions can help or hurt your fictional career.

6

SAVE YOUR BEST-PHOTO SOUVENIR

If you've published a photo post, tap SAVE MY BEST POST ↗ during the game or final voting to download a personalized keepsake. Do this before the winner is announced. Original uploaded photos and videos are deleted after the reveal.

7

VOTE FOR THE ULTIMATE INFLUENCER

When final voting opens, choose another player's name. You cannot vote for yourself. You may change your vote before voting closes. Then watch the winner reveal.

THE HOUSE RULES

BE FAMOUS. NOT MEAN.

  • Make fun of influencer culture, not the people playing.
  • Be creative, dramatic, and absurd. The money and followers are imaginary.
  • Use photos and videos you're comfortable sharing with everyone in the room.
  • No actual social media accounts are needed.
  • One person hosts; everyone else plays on their own device.
REMEMBER: Your online fame is fictional. Your commitment to the bit should be very real.

THE FAME GAME · YOUR GUIDE TO GLORY

GO VIRAL.
GET RICH.
GET CANCELED.

Become an Influencer is a multiplayer party game about the ridiculous world of internet fame. Invent an outrageous online persona, create posts, collect engagement, survive the algorithm, and compete to become the ultimate influencer. No real social media account required.

01

JOIN THE FEED

Scan your host's QR code or open the game link and enter the room code. Choose an influencer handle and persona. One person hosts; everyone else plays on their own phone.

02

INTRODUCE YOURSELF

Are you a luxury influencer with no money? A wellness expert with dubious advice? A conspiracy creator with suspiciously devoted followers? Give the feed your unforgettable introduction.

03

POST YOUR WAY TO FAME

Follow the creative challenges or invent your own posts. Write captions, share photos or videos, announce fake brand deals, launch bizarre trends, and start fictional drama. Post as often as you like.

04

WORK THE ALGORITHM

Like and comment on other players' posts. Engagement earns followers, credibility, imaginary earnings, and notoriety. Your two highest-engagement posts per round count toward your score.

05

SURVIVE THE DRAMA

Unexpected influencer dilemmas may appear. Make choices that could boost your fame, damage your credibility, or turn a tiny scandal into your entire brand.

06

VOTE FOR THE ULTIMATE INFLUENCER

When the game reaches the final vote, choose who deserves the crown. You cannot vote for yourself. The winner is revealed when voting closes.

07

SAVE YOUR SOUVENIR

If you shared a photo, tap Save My Best Post before the winner is announced to download a keepsake featuring your most popular photo post. Uploaded photos and videos are deleted after the winner reveal.

THE ONLY REAL RULE: Be wildly creative, not cruel. Make fun of influencer culture, not the people playing. Your fame is fictional. Your comedic legacy is forever.
